🧠 SKILLSWAP DECISION ENGINE

Explainable marketplace decisions.

SkillSwap uses explicit rules for rejection, booking conflicts and service discovery rather than pretending every decision comes from an unexplained AI model.

DECISION POINT 01

Creator rejection

When a creator declines a request, the request becomes Declined. The user is not left at a dead end: SkillSwap can surface similar services so the project can continue.

DECISION POINT 02

Double-booking protection

A service with an active Pending or Accepted request cannot receive another active booking request. This protects the creator from conflicting commitments.

DECISION POINT 03

Explainable discovery

Recommended services use deterministic relevance signals. Users can see a “Why this match?” explanation rather than receiving an unexplained ranking.
